6. Cancellations and refunds

  • Before we start cooking: you can cancel for a full refund. Call or text us as soon as you can.
  • After we start cooking: we cannot cancel or refund, because the food is made to order.
  • If something is wrong (a missing or wrong item, food that is not as described, or a quality problem), tell us within 24 hours of receiving your order. A photo helps. We will remake the item, give you a credit, or refund the affected item, whichever we think is fair.
  • If you change your mind or do not like a dish that was made as described, we are not required to give a refund, but we will always listen.
  • Refunds go back to the way you paid. Card refunds can take 5 to 10 business days to appear. Zelle refunds are sent back through Zelle.
  • A delivery fee is not refunded once the courier has been booked, unless the problem was our mistake.

7. Allergens and food safety

Please read this if you or anyone you are feeding has a food allergy.

  • Our kitchen prepares food that contains or comes into contact with the major food allergens: milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy and sesame.
  • For example: dishes with fish or shrimp (such as Kisinia, grilled fish and salmon) contain fish or shellfish, Zege contains eggs, and chapati and samosas contain wheat. This is only a few examples, not a full list of ingredients.
  • Even a dish that does not list an allergen can come into contact with it in our kitchen, on our equipment or in our oil. We cannot guarantee that any dish is free of any allergen.
  • If you have an allergy, call or text us before you order at 832-977-0178, and mention it in the notes box at checkout. If we cannot make the dish safely, we will tell you. If you are not sure it is safe for you, please do not order it.

Food safety. Eating raw or undercooked meat, poultry, seafood or eggs can raise your risk of food-borne illness, especially for young children, older adults, pregnant people and anyone with a weakened immune system. Our food is cooked fresh and is ready to eat when you receive it. Please keep it hot or cold as appropriate, reheat it until steaming hot, and refrigerate leftovers promptly.
