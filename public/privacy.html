Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version. We collect only what we need to make and deliver your order. We do not sell your personal information. We do not run ads or tracking cookies. A few trusted companies handle things like payments, delivery and hosting for us, and this page lists them.

1. What we collect

  • What you give us: your name, phone number, delivery address (for delivery), the items you order, the day and time you want your order, and any notes you type. If you create an account, your email address and a password (your password is handled by our login service, and we do not see it).
  • Payment details: card and Apple Pay payments are entered on Stripe's page, so we never see your card number. We receive a record that the payment went through, the amount, and your email if you give it to Stripe. For Zelle, your bank handles the payment, and we may see the name and amount on the payment.
  • Automatic information: like most websites, our hosting service records basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open.
  • Your phone number may be checked with a phone lookup service to confirm it is a real, working number, and your address may be checked with mapping services to confirm it is a real address.

2. How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and confirm your order, and to contact you about it by phone or text (for example, to confirm the time, ask a question, or tell you about a problem).
  • To take payments and prevent fraud, spam and abuse.
  • To keep your account and order history if you have one, and to remember your cart and details on your device so you do not have to type them again.
  • To keep business records, handle tax and legal requirements, and resolve disputes.
  • We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not send marketing texts. If we ever want to send promotions, we will ask for your permission first.

3. Calls and text messages

When you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your order, such as confirmations, timing, questions and problems. These are not marketing messages. Message and data rates from your phone company may apply. If you would rather not get texts, tell us and we will contact you another way when we can.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with companies that help us run the business, and only what they need to do their job:

  • Stripe: card and Apple Pay payments.
  • Delivery courier service (Uber Direct): your name, phone number, delivery address and what is in the order, so a driver can bring it to you.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.
  • Supabase: stores accounts and order history.
  • Twilio, Resend and similar services: phone number checks, and text, email or push alerts that tell us about a new order.
  • Google and the US Census Bureau: address checks. Google also supplies the fonts this site uses, which means your browser contacts Google to load them.
  • Google Sheets: we may keep a simple order log in a private spreadsheet.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to respond to a legal request, to protect someone's safety, or if the business is sold or transferred. These companies have their own privacy policies for how they handle information they collect directly from you.

5. What is stored on your device

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It does save a few things in your browser's local storage so it works the way you expect: your cart, your favorite dishes, the details you used on your last order, and your login if you are signed in. You can clear these any time in your browser settings. If you do, you will be signed out and your cart will be emptied.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for bookkeeping, taxes, resolving problems and following the law, which is often several years. Account details are kept until you ask us to delete the account. After that we delete or anonymize information we no longer need.

7. Your choices

  • You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. We may keep some records if the law requires, or if we need them to handle a dispute.
  • You can browse the menu and check out as a guest without creating an account.
  • To make a request, call or text us at the number below. We may ask a question or two to confirm it is really you before we change or share anything.

8. Keeping your information safe
